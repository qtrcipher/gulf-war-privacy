# Gulf War — Support

**Last updated:** April 22, 2026

Need help with **Gulf War**? You're in the right place. This page answers the most common questions and tells you how to reach the developer directly.

## Contact

If you can't find what you need below, email **dev.hamam@gmail.com**. Please include:

- Your **device model** (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro)
- Your **iOS version** (Settings → General → About)
- The **app version and build** (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen in-game, or in the App Store listing)
- A short description of the problem — screenshots or screen recordings help a lot

We try to reply within **2–3 business days**.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

1. Sign in to the **same Apple ID** you used to make the purchase.
2. Open Gulf War and go to **Settings → Restore Purchases**.
3. Wait for the confirmation message. Your Remove Ads, Premium Unlock, or coin packs will be re-applied.

If nothing is restored:

- Confirm you're signed into the correct Apple ID (Settings → [your name] on iOS).
- Confirm the original purchase appears in your Apple purchase history (Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → Purchase History).
- If it does, email us with the approximate purchase date and we'll investigate.

### My progress disappeared after reinstalling the app

Gulf War stores progress **on your device** rather than on a server. This means:

- Deleting the app **deletes your local save** (level unlocks, coin balance, settings).
- **Game Center data** (leaderboards, achievements) persists because Apple stores it.
- **Purchases** persist because Apple stores them — use Restore Purchases as above.

To avoid losing progress in the future, keep the app installed and enable **Offload Unused Apps** (Settings → App Store) instead of deleting, which preserves local data.

### Why am I seeing ads even though I bought Remove Ads?

Try the following in order:

1. **Restore Purchases** from Settings → Restore Purchases.
2. **Restart the app** fully (swipe it out of the app switcher, then reopen).
3. Ensure you're signed into the **Apple ID that made the purchase**.
4. If the issue persists, email us with your purchase date and Apple ID region.

### The game crashes or freezes

1. Force-quit and reopen the app (swipe up from the bottom and flick Gulf War away).
2. Restart your device.
3. Update to the latest iOS version (Settings → General → Software Update).
4. Make sure you're on the latest version of Gulf War (App Store → your profile → Update).

If the crash still happens, email us with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of what you were doing when it crashed.

### Audio doesn't play / the music stopped

- Check your ringer/silent switch and device volume.
- Go to **Settings → Audio** in the game and confirm the music/SFX volume sliders aren't at zero.
- If another app (phone call, Siri, Music) interrupted audio, fully quit and reopen Gulf War.

### I can't sign into Game Center / my scores aren't submitting

- Open iOS **Settings → Game Center** and confirm you're signed in.
- Some scores are queued locally when offline and submitted the next time Gulf War launches while online — give it a minute and relaunch.
- If Game Center is signed in but scores still don't appear, email us.

### How do I change the language?

Open **Settings → Language** inside the app and select English or Arabic (العربية). The app restarts to apply the new language. The selection is also tied to your iOS system language on first launch.

### How do I delete my data?

Because Gulf War does not operate a server, **uninstalling the app** deletes all local data (progress, settings, coin balance). To remove:

- **Game Center scores/achievements** — contact [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/game-center).
- **Purchase history** — Apple retains this for receipt/restore functionality; see Apple's policies.
- **AdMob data** — go to [Google's My Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/) or reset your IDFA at Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

For full details, see the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/).

### Is Gulf War safe for children?

Gulf War is rated **9+** on the App Store. The game contains cartoon-style aerial combat with no blood, gore, or realistic violence. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/#4-childrens-privacy) for our approach to children's privacy.

### How do I report a bug?

Email **dev.hamam@gmail.com** with:

- What happened vs. what you expected
- Steps to reproduce
- Device model + iOS version + app version
- A screenshot or screen recording if possible

### How do I suggest a feature?

Feature requests are welcome — send them to **dev.hamam@gmail.com**. We read every one, though we can't promise to implement them all.
